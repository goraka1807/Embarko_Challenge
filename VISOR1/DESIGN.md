## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of bespoke industrial design, high-end automotive telemetry, and cinematic sci-fi minimalism. Engineered for mission-critical command environments and executive interfaces, it replaces noisy speculative UI tropes with surgical precision, restraint, and tactical poise.

### Target Audience & Persona
Built for aerospace executives, research engineers, and operators who require instant situational comprehension without decorative visual clutter. Every pixel communicates poise, quiet power, and hyper-reliable machinery.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Restrained Holography:** Illumination is treated as an energy state, not decoration. Glows are strictly directional, tightly calibrated, and reserved for focal readouts.
- **Architectural Dark Space:** Deep obsidian voids let 3D spatial models and telemetry modules breathe with high-end gallery presence.
- **Micro-Machined Precision:** Interfaces feel physically milled, featuring ultra-fine hairline dividers, tactile radius transitions, and calibrated digital instruments.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep optical base with brushed-metallic mid-tones and disciplined photonic accents.

- **Obsidian & Graphite Foundation:** Base environments rely on `#040608` (void base), `#080b0f` (viewport canvas), and `#12171f` (milled structural surface).
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Surface separators leverage `#182433` and `#1f2a38` for razor-sharp visual containment without chromatic pollution.
- **Photonic Accents:** `#00e5ff` (Electric Cyan) is restricted to active power states, dynamic focal points, and live vector nodes. `#38bdf8` (Ice Blue) designates secondary telemetry and balanced state feedback.
- **Diagnostic Alert:** `#f59e0b` (Muted Amber) flags thermal deviations, system thresholds, and overrides, maintaining critical awareness without harsh alarm fatigue.
- **Text Tiers:** Primary values and titles leverage `#f1f5f9` (crystalline white), secondary data sets utilize `#94a3b8` (machined silver), and resting captions sink into `#475569` (slate carbon).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances modern engineering clarity with technical legibility:

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Section Titles):** Delivers sculptural geometric structure with subtle technical idiosyncrasies. Used strictly for status headings, module titles, and primary readouts.
- **Sora (Analytical Body & Context):** Optimized for continuous readability under stressful operational scenarios. Used for situational briefs, system descriptions, and telemetry transcripts.
- **Inter (Telemetry & Values):** Monospaced precision reserved for metric readouts, timecodes, coordinates, and status tokens. Enhanced letter-spacing ensures distinct character isolation at high speeds.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column dynamic fluid grid architecture structured around a rigid 4px micro-unit system.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, 2.5rem canvas margins, and 1.5rem gutters. Viewports allocate large, unobstructed observation zones for real-time visual assets, keeping control arrays and telemetry docks anchored to perimeter zones.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem margins and 1rem gutters. Secondary instrument panels collapse into bottom-drawer panels or swipeable auxiliary heads.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem safe margins and 0.75rem gutters. Floating modules reflow into stacked contextual cards with swipeable pill navigators.
- **Rhythm:** Interior component padding exclusively leverages `space-xs` (4px) through `space-xl` (40px) to maintain predictable alignment across dense instrumentation grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering avoids heavy drop shadows and glowing neon hazes, adopting instead a physical milled-substrate metaphor:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Floor):** `#040608` acting as pure negative space behind 3D components and system schematics.
- **Level 1 (Structural Canvas):** `#080b0f` base for dashboard frames, top bars, and status shelves.
- **Level 2 (Milled Containers):** `#12171f` panels framed with 1px `#182433` hairline borders.
- **Level 3 (Focused & Interactive Layers):** `#18202b` surfaces with subtle top-lit radial gradients (e.g., `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.03) 0%, transparent 70%)`) and razor-sharp `#1f2a38` borders.
- **Level 4 (Photonic Interaction):** Active overlays and floating palettes employ subtle backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with ultra-fine `#00e5ff33` containment outlines.

## Shapes

The geometric framework mirrors high-performance automotive and aerospace cockpit interfaces:

- **Tactile Pills (`rounded-full` / `rounded-xl`):** Primary action triggers, mode switches, and live status pills feature sweeping radial terminal ends for effortless visual tracking and physical thumb target recognition.
- **Engineered Panels (`rounded-lg`):** Instrument cards, diagnostic monitors, and modal containers use precise 16px to 24px radii to balance structural discipline with aerodynamic ergonomics.
- **Circular Indicators:** Precision telemetry rings, progress halos, and core status hubs employ pure circular geometry to focus attention during critical operations.

## Components

### Action Triggers (Pill Buttons)
- **Primary:** Tactile pill container surfaced with `#18202b` and a gentle directional top-sheen. Highlighted with a 1px `#00e5ff` boundary and crisp `#00e5ff` typography. Hover states introduce a subtle `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)` inner wash.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#12171f`, hairline outline `#1f2a38`, and text `#94a3b8`. Under hover, the border shifts smoothly to `#38bdf8`.

### Status Indicators & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped modules rendered in `#0c1015` with 1px borders in `#182433`.
- Incorporates a 6px solid circular LED beacon: pulsing `#00e5ff` for online states, steady `#38bdf8` for standby, and `#f59e0b` for caution. Typography uses `label-sm` in Inter.

### Telemetry Gauges & Circular Readouts
- Minimal hairline tracks (`#182433`, 1.5px stroke width) overlaid with energetic `#00e5ff` or `#38bdf8` segment arcs.
- Center displays feature prominent `headline-md` metrics paired with uppercase `label-sm` unit indicators below.

### Cards & Module Shelves
- Assembled from Level 2 milled surfaces (`#12171f`), zero dropshadows, and 1px crisp borders (`#182433`).
- Headers showcase uppercase tracking (`label-md`) with subtle divider tracks separating the operational canvas from metadata summaries.

### Input Fields
- Recessed `#080b0f` containers with 1px `#182433` borders and `rounded-lg` geometry.
- On focus, transition border color to `#00e5ff` with a restrained 1px inset boundary. Monospaced placeholder text in `#475569`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios are concentric engineered rings: `#12171f` outer ring with 1px `#1f2a38` perimeter, activating a solid `#00e5ff` central core when toggled.
- Checkboxes feature precision chamfered squares with cyan-accented micro-check marks upon selection.
